# Repeated Cross Sections: Unsupported

DGJT panel LP-DiD uses within-unit outcome differences, not differences between
unrelated respondents. Neither auto mode without unit nor explicit rcs is accepted.

A cluster-period mean could be treated as a new panel outcome only under a
separately specified stable-population sampling model, with appropriate population
weights, within-cell sampling uncertainty, and a clearly distinct cluster-level
target. It is not generally the respondent-level ATT and is not derived or
certified here. We therefore provide no implicit RCS conversion.
